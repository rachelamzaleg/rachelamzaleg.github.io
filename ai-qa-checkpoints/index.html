AI-assisted QA · Safeguards

Evidence Before Conclusions

The assistant may prepare and examine evidence. It does not decide whether to ignore a failure, change a live system or publish a conclusion.

Prove, then investigate

I establish a clean baseline before testing a change. When something fails, I verify the claim, reproduce when possible and compare working against failing behavior. Suspected causes stay hypotheses until supported by evidence.

First real failure = checkpoint. Capture what happened, show the blocker and wait for direction instead of inventing a workaround.

What requires my approval?

  • Changing live configuration, deleting or overwriting data, or publishing results to a tracker or stakeholder.
  • Running browser automation against a live system.
  • Deciding whether unexpected behavior is a defect or intentional behavior.

Routine drafting and read-only checks can proceed without unnecessary interruptions.

Review work others will see

For a stakeholder-facing artifact, I use a separate validation pass to challenge assumptions, missing coverage and contradictions. The validator reports gaps; it does not silently rewrite the evidence. Lightweight internal notes do not need the same process.
