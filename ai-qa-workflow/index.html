AI-assisted QA · The workflow

How I Work with AI in QA

AI helps me move from an unclear change to focused, evidence-based QA. I keep ownership of scope, judgment and approvals.

The six-step QA flow

  1. PrepareConfirm requirements and a working baseline.
  2. AnalyzeUnderstand the change and affected components.
  3. ScopePrioritize risks, negative cases and boundaries.
  4. DraftWrite executable, customer-oriented test cases.
  5. CheckRun live checks and capture actual evidence.
  6. ReportRecord results using live tracker information.
Something off? Stop and show the evidence. I choose the next step; the assistant does not quietly work around the failure.

Explore one principle at a time

Evidence & review checkpoints →When to verify, stop and ask for approval. The improvement loop →Turn a correction into a reusable rule. Focused AI roles →Separate drafting from validation and reporting. Reusable runbooks →Keep only the verified working procedure.
