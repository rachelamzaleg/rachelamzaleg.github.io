Build a QA Improvement Loop

Turn recurring QA problems into clear, tested rules that improve the next task.

Follow the improvement loop

  1. NoticeFind a recurring mistake or repeated task.
  2. UnderstandIdentify the missing rule or weak procedure.
  3. ImproveUpdate one relevant skill, reference or instruction.
  4. VerifyTest the new behavior and commit the correction.
Useful rule: “If baseline setup fails, capture the error and stop for direction.” Specific, testable instructions are better than general reminders.

Keep knowledge in one place

Workspace instructions contain common rules. Skills contain repeatable procedures. References contain current product facts. Ticket notes contain specific findings. Live trackers remain the source of status.

Read relevant sections and targeted log excerpts rather than entire histories.
