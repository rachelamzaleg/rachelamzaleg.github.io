Give Claude a Role

Give AI-assisted QA tasks distinct responsibilities and explicit review gates.

1. Assign a focused role

CoordinatorControls scope and checkpoints.
DocumentationDrafts from evidence.
ValidatorFlags unsupported claims and gaps.
QA TesterRuns authorized checks.
StatusUses current tracker results.

2. Separate drafting from validation

Have an independent pass report specific issues without silently rewriting findings. Reserve critical judgments for human review.

Draft → Validate → Review → Finalize

3. Keep small tasks simple

Use a separate validator for stakeholder-facing deliverables and complex test plans, not every internal question.

Validator prompt

Validate this QA draft against the provided evidence.
List unsupported assumptions, missing cases, contradictions and open questions.
Do not invent expected behavior or silently rewrite the draft.
Creation and validation are separate responsibilities.
